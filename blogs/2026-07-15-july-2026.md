Production-Aligned Evaluations in Simulation Runs — GenerativeAgent Simulation runs now surface the same evaluations used in production, aligning pre-deployment testing with production monitoring. Each run shows Conversation Monitoring flags, Goal Completion, and Custom Evals where configured. Builders can view goal completion and mistake rates across simulation runs, and see detailed mistake flags and goal completion status and rationale for every run, in addition to the pass/fail summary.
